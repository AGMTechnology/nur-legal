Nūr teaches the Arabic script, Qur'anic recitation, stories and manners. We take

**We do not ship Qur'anic text, recitation, quiz questions or teaching content

**Guidance articles for parents are our own editorial writing and are not part
of that review.** Where we say our Qur'an content is scholar-reviewed, that

Recitation audio is the murattal of Shaykh Maḥmūd Khalīl al-Ḥuṣarī, sourced from
QUL. Attributions for every audio and art source are listed in the app.

**If you find an error in any Qur'anic or religious content, please tell us at
