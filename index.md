## The short version

Nūr works without an account. Nothing about your child is required to use it.

Your child's recitation recordings **never leave your phone** — not to us, not to
anyone. There are no ads, no ad identifiers, and no behavioural tracking of
children. A child never gets an account; only a parent does.

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.
